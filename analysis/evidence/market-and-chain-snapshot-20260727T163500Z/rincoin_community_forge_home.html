About

The Story of Rincoin

Rincoin emerged from a simple but powerful belief: cryptocurrency mining should remain accessible to everyone. In an era increasingly dominated by ASIC mining farms and corporate interests, Rincoin returns to the original vision of decentralized digital money.

Rincoin uses a triple-layer hashing sequence — BLAKE3 → Argon2d → SHA3-256 — with a memory-hard stage designed to raise the cost of specialized mining hardware. No premine, no ICO. There is no single Rincoin authority.

Technology

Built on Innovation

Rincoin combines proven blockchain principles with modern cryptographic innovation to deliver a secure, fair, and truly decentralized network.

Memory-hard RinHash

RinHash combines BLAKE3, Argon2d and SHA3-256. Its memory-hard stage is designed to increase the cost of specialized mining hardware; specialization resistance is parameter-dependent and evaluated empirically.

Proof of Work

Proof-of-Work consensus secures the deployed network through computational work.

Public infrastructure

Community Forge operates Fulcrum endpoints, explorer and wallet infrastructure supporting the network.

Capped-supply design

The original whitepaper defines a 168 million RIN target. A subsidy-reconciliation proposal is in preparation for public discussion.

Decentralized

No premine and no central authority. There is no single Rincoin authority; adoption is a decision of network participants.

Open Source

Open-source software maintained by Rincoin Community Forge. Anyone can review and verify the code.

Tokenomics

Simple, Mine-First Economics

Rincoin follows a distribution-first model: no premine, no ICO, and network issuance through Proof of Work. The focus is transparent emission and broad participation over private allocations.

No Premine

No founder reserve was minted before public mining.

No ICO

No public token sale or private fundraising tranche.

PoW Distribution

New RIN enters circulation through mining rewards.

Fixed-Supply Goal

Issuance follows a capped-supply design with predictable schedule updates.

Capped-supply design

The original RinCoin whitepaper defines a 168 million RIN monetary target. A technically reviewed proposal to reconcile the future subsidy schedule with that target is being prepared for public discussion.
