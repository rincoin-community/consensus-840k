**Rincoin is approaching a consensus decision at height 840,000. This notice does not announce that a chain split is final or inevitable.**

Our first preference is one common consensus and one chain if technical agreement is still possible. We invite maintainers of every active Rincoin implementation to begin a focused technical discussion of the consensus rule, implementation behavior, activation, compatibility tests, and timing.

**The seven-day response window asks only for confirmation of willingness to enter that substantive discussion.** It is not a deadline to negotiate or complete an agreement. If a common consensus is not possible, the next preference is to coordinate non-colliding technical identifiers and activation handling so that nodes, miners, pools, wallets, exchanges, information services, and users are not exposed to avoidable chain mixing.

If neither form of coordination succeeds, a precise, independently testable fork-separation specification will be published before any related signalling or enforcement release. Silence will be recorded only as no reply, never as agreement or consent.

Technical documents, as well as economic rationale, scenario comparison, and the whitepaper draft after consensus change are maintained at https://github.com/rincoin-community/consensus-840k. They will be published in the following hours/days and discussed there.

**Please confirm through the route above by the deadline whether you are willing to begin this technical discussion.**
